# Amaura CLI — Full Product Specification

An evidence-first reliability layer for AI agents.

## Mission
Independently examine AI-generated work and its claims, collect reproducible evidence, challenge weak conclusions, and prevent unsupported success reports from being treated as verified results.

## Defining Principle
> Never confuse an agent's assertion with evidence that the assertion is true.

## Core Questions Answered
1. What exactly was claimed?
2. What evidence would establish or contradict each claim?
3. What checks were actually executed, and in what environment?
4. What did the evidence establish, fail to establish, or contradict?
5. What remains uncertain, and what would be needed to resolve it?
